MothLights Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

MothLights is developed and operated by Danila Komarov (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the MothLights iOS game and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

MothLights stores your best score and sound preference locally on your device so the game can remember them between sessions. We do not receive a copy of this local data on a developer-operated server. You can remove local app data by deleting MothLights from your device.

Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, MothLights submits completed game scores to Apple Game Center and can show you its leaderboard. Apple processes Game Center account and gameplay information to provide this service. Your nickname, avatar, and leaderboard scores may be visible to other Game Center players according to the service and your settings. We do not receive your Apple Account password or maintain a separate server copy of your scores. For details about Apple’s handling of Game Center information, see Apple’s Game Center privacy information.

In-app purchases

Optional continues are purchased through Apple’s StoreKit and App Store payment systems. Apple processes payment information and provides the app with a transaction result so it can deliver the purchased continue. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple handles purchase records, refunds, and payment settings under its own policies and account controls.

Instagram link

MothLights includes a link to the public Instagram account @mothlights_game. If you choose to open it, Instagram (Meta) may receive information about your visit under its own privacy policy. MothLights does not send your game score or other personal information to Instagram through this link.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version of MothLights does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and we do not use the app to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or location.

Retention and privacy choices

Your best score and sound preference remain on your device until you delete the app or its local data. Game Center scores and purchase records are handled and retained by Apple under its applicable services and policies. You can manage Game Center and purchase settings through your Apple Account and contact Apple for requests concerning data held by Apple. We do not have a developer-operated account or score database to search or delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the effective date.
